Maintenance Windows

We keep disruption to a minimum with predictable, well-communicated maintenance schedules.

Scheduled Maintenance

Routine maintenance is performed during low-traffic windows, typically Sundays 2:00–6:00 AM ET. Downtime during scheduled windows does not count against the SLA.

72-Hour Advance Notice

All scheduled maintenance is communicated at least 72 hours in advance via email, in-app banner, and our status page so your team can plan accordingly.

Emergency Maintenance

In rare cases requiring emergency patches (security vulnerabilities, critical bugs), we provide as much notice as possible and post real-time updates on our status page.
